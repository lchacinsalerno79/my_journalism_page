Introducing Laura Chacín

About Me

I'm a Venezuelan journalist based in New York City and a graduate student in Bilingual Journalism at the Craig Newmark Graduate School of Journalism at CUNY. I'm interested in investigative journalism, immigration, and stories about Latino communities in New York.

My Three Favorite Journalism Stories

  1. Venezuelans Sent to El Salvador's CECOT Prison - ProPublica

    I chose this investigation because it gives a voice to Venezuelan families affected by immigration policies. It shows the importance of investigative journalism in exposing human rights issues.

  2. Migrar, el único camino - El Universal

    I like how this story uses multimedia storytelling to explore the experiences of migrants. It is a powerful example of how journalism can combine visual elements and personal narratives.

  3. One Corner. Seven Arrests. A Community Changed. - Documented

    I admire how this investigation combines on-the-ground reporting, video evidence, and personal stories to show how immigration enforcement affects an entire community.
